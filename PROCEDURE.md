    **Reading, with the evidence.** No model recites the 1966 article: 0 of 15 everywhere, the direct test. The contamination signal (nats per token) rises with size in both families, but the matched pairs do not agree on a family: 135M above 160m (+0.06 vs 0.00), 410m above 360M (+0.18 vs +0.13), 1.7B above 1b (+0.33 vs +0.22). By the PRESPEC's own reading rule, no family effect is reported. The per-line files show why the signal grows: the fixed lines that beat the reference most are short ordinary questions (WHAT DO YOU THINK, WHAT IS IT YOU REALLY WANT TO KNOW, CAN YOU THINK OF A SPECIFIC EXAMPLE), and only 1 to 3 of the 87 fixed lines exceed the largest reference margin on any model, which is what fluent English looks like, not a memorized string. The lowercase form is cheaper than the capitals on every model (median 0.9 to 1.8 nats per token), so the script's shouting is itself a source of surprise that the arms will have to learn. Conclusion for the record: at zero-shot, the DOCTOR script is not detectably in any of the seven models by probe-v1, and Krystal Martinez's K69 conjecture (ELIZA material concentrated in code sources giving SmolLM2 an edge) is not supported by this measurement; it remains testable in the trained arms as a family difference in lift on framing A at matched data.
